# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels

SEV1 is a full outage or data exposure: the on-call engineer must respond within 15 minutes. SEV2 is a major degradation: respond within 1 hour. SEV3 is minor and handled in business hours.

## On-call rotation

The on-call rotation changes every Monday at 09:00. The current on-call engineer is listed in the status channel topic.

## Postmortems

A written postmortem is required for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident and must be published within 5 business days. Postmortems are blameless.
