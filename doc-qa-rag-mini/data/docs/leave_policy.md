# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and requests are approved by your line manager at least one week in advance.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is paid. A doctor's note is required when an absence lasts more than 2 consecutive days. Please notify your manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers to 4 weeks. Parental leave can be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.
